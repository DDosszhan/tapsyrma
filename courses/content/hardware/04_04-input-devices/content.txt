## Input Devices and Touchscreen Technologies

### Learning goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to choose suitable input devices for a task and explain how resistive, capacitive and infrared touchscreens detect touch.

### Why this matters

A computer needs input to receive information. Different input devices are designed for different tasks: scanning a product code, recording sound, capturing a 3D object or detecting a touch.

### Step 1: What counts as input?

An input device sends data or instructions into a computer system. Choose devices by the kind of data needed and the context.

### Step 2: Common devices

Keyboard enters text; optical mouse detects movement; microphone captures sound; digital camera captures images; barcode/QR scanners read coded patterns; 2D scanners capture flat documents, while 3D scanners capture object shape.

[diagram:input-families]

### Step 3: Resistive touchscreens

Two conductive layers contact when pressed. They can work with many kinds of stylus or gloves but require pressure.

### Step 4: Capacitive touchscreens

A conductive touch changes the electrical field/capacitance on the screen. Light fingertip interaction and multitouch are common.

### Step 5: Infrared touchscreens

An array of infrared beams lies across the screen surface. A touch interrupts beams, allowing the touch position to be detected.

[diagram:touch-types]

### Worked example 1: Shop checkout

A barcode scanner reads the code printed on a product. The system looks up the product record and price; the barcode usually identifies the item rather than storing every product detail.

### Worked example 2: Museum information kiosk

A large public touchscreen could use infrared sensors. Users interrupt the light-beam grid to select options without needing a conductive finger.

### Common mistakes

Tip: A barcode scanner usually reads a product identifier; the system then looks up its price.

Tip: A touchscreen accepts input and displays output.

Tip: A 3D scanner captures an object's shape; a 3D printer builds an object.

### Quick recap

Input devices enter information. Match the data and environment to the device. Touchscreens can be resistive, capacitive or infrared.

### Apply your knowledge

Choose the most suitable input device for a passport checkpoint, a voice note app and a product checkout. Explain your choices.
